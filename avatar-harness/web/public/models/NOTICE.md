## Cheng / 澄 — original procedural character

`cheng.glb` is generated by this project's `web/src/characters/cheng.ts` using the existing Three.js dependency. No third-party character mesh, texture, Blender installation, or model weights are used. Geometry was produced with AI coding assistance.

This is a stylized bust with head/neck transform nodes and five named facial morph channels, not a complete skinned full-body avatar or ARKit 52 model. The built-in idle head motion and blinks are local procedural animation, not inferred speech animation. No additional third-party character-asset license applies; library licenses remain unchanged.

## LAM local avatar and speech model

As of 2026-10-31, `lam/avatar.zip` is generated by the official LAM-20K service on data2 from the Colorado 2022 photograph of Elon Musk (U.S. Air Force / Trevor Cokley), using the crop at https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Elon_Musk_Colorado_2022_(cropped_2).jpg . The source photograph is a US federal government work, public domain in the United States. The generated avatar is an experimental reconstruction, not a recording of the person. Its published SHA-256 is `5da20bf3ebde99f2cf2bbc1934674e1f41204bbb13970f0b7b3eadfc269ba3bb`; source ZIP SHA-256 is `324805f6c556a02bae9278b9f7982284390b07149a0d05feb08b66a0568f0ec7`. LAM code, model weights and FLAME dependencies retain their separate upstream terms; see `sources.lock.json` and `docs/data2-models.md`. The skin has 51 named morph channels (no tongueOut), and seven supplied animations are selected/reordered for the harness. Their sustained head-pitch bias is removed, retaining nod/turn variation. Geometry, morphs and other ZIP member contents are unchanged by that correction. Source provenance and the previous user-supplied asset backup are recorded in `assets/characters/lam/README.md` and `current.json` in the project.

`lam-a2e/lam_ios.onnx` and `.data` are a community Wav2ARKit ONNX conversion from https://huggingface.co/adolon-ai/lam-a2e, declared Apache-2.0. Original config and provenance are retained in that directory. The conversion maintainer records the exact original training checkpoint/fine-tuner as unknown; do not equate these files with a verified official LAM checkpoint. Current mapping follows alphabetical LAM channel order, not the Apple order listed in the community config.
